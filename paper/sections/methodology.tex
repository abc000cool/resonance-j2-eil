\section{Simulation methodology}\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Numerical truth and cross-validation}

The truth is a two-spacecraft Cartesian propagator with $J_2$--$J_4$ zonal
gravity, integrated with DOP853 (a verified fixed-step RK4 fast path,
agreeing to 3\,\textmu m relative position over an orbit, is used inside
the Monte-Carlo campaign). A zonal-only field is deliberate: the study
isolates the perturbation the analytic models claim to capture, so any
closed-loop pathology is attributable to the
estimation-and-control architecture rather than to unmodeled forces.

The propagator is cross-validated against an independent oracle: an Orekit
numerical propagator with a degree-4, order-0 gravity field, from identical
initial conditions over the full 30-day campaign duration
(Table~\ref{tab:orekit}). Relative position --- the quantity this study
uses --- agrees to 2.3~mm RMS (6.9~mm maximum), five orders of magnitude
below the formation scale. Absolute position agrees to 9.1~m RMS; the
divergence is consistent with the $\sim$$10^{-9}$ relative difference
between the two libraries' gravitational-constant conventions, growing
quadratically in time, and is reported for completeness.

\subsection{Scenario and campaign design}

The baseline scenario is a near-circular chief at
$a = 7078.137$~km ($\sim$700~km altitude), $e = 0.001$, $i = 98^\circ$,
with 1-km formations, a 30-day duration, and a 60-s step for measurement,
control, and filtering. A trial is declared \emph{diverged} when the
formation-keeping error exceeds a bound of $100\times$ the formation size;
diverged trials are excluded from delta-V statistics and tallied as
failures.

The campaign runs in two tiers. The \emph{screening} tier replaces the
numerical truth with an STM-surrogate truth (KGD plus calibrated white
acceleration) and sweeps the full grid --- 3 controllers $\times$ 3 filter
models $\times$ 18 (architecture, $\sigma$) points $\times$ 4 geometries
$=$ 648 points at 20 seeds each (12{,}960 trials) --- cheaply. The
\emph{full} tier reruns the screening-informed grid (sigma points pruned to
bracket the observed knees; the redundant GERO geometry dropped) against
the numerical truth at $N = 100$ seeds: 324 grid points plus five
extended-sigma backfill points, 32{,}900 trials. Agreement between tiers
validates the surrogate: per-cell median delta-V rank-correlates at
Spearman $\rho = 0.83$ with numerical-truth medians $7\%$ higher at the
median (Pearson correlation is uninformative at 0.37 --- the delta-V
distribution is heavy-tailed, and rank statistics are used throughout).

Every trial is exactly reproducible: the RNG seed derives from
\texttt{SeedSequence([root, point-hash, trial-index])}, so any single trial
can be regenerated in isolation; results are written per grid point to
Parquet, making campaigns resumable and incrementally extensible.

\subsection{Knee metric}\label{sec:kneemetric}

The navigation-accuracy ``knee'' --- the accuracy beyond which further
sensor improvement no longer buys delta-V --- is reported with a metric
designed after a naive version (first $\sigma$ whose median delta-V exceeds
$1.1\times$ the tightest-sigma baseline) proved fragile on a coarse sigma
grid, agreeing between tiers in only 55\% of cells. Per
(controller, architecture, filter, geometry) cell the robust metric
reports: a \emph{status} --- rising, flat, or falling, from the Spearman
trend of median delta-V over feasible sigma points, where a sigma point is
feasible if at least 10 of 100 seeds survive --- so that ``no knee'' is
distinguishable from ``trend inverted'' and ``all trials diverged'';
threshold knees at 5/10/25/50\% delta-V rise with a stability flag (the
verdict is \emph{stable} when the 5\% and 50\% knees agree within one grid
step); and a fitted knee from
$\Delta V(\sigma) = \Delta V_0\,(1 + (\sigma/\sigma_k)^p)$, flagged when it
extrapolates beyond the swept range. Infeasible sigma points are marked
explicitly in every figure rather than silently omitted.

\subsection{Reproducibility}

The implementation is pure Python (NumPy/SciPy/CasADi), with a locked
dependency environment (\texttt{uv}), a Docker fallback, continuous
integration running the full verification suite, and a figure DAG that
regenerates every figure and table in this paper from the committed
Parquet data with one command. Code and data are openly released; the
archived version of record is linked in the data-availability statement.
