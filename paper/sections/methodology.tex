\section{Simulation methodology}\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Numerical truth and cross-validation}

The truth is a two-spacecraft Cartesian propagator with $J_2$--$J_4$ zonal
gravity, integrated with DOP853; a verified fixed-step RK4 fast path,
agreeing to 3\,\textmu m relative position over an orbit, is used inside
the Monte-Carlo campaign. A zonal-only field is deliberate. The study
isolates the perturbation the analytic models claim to capture, so any
closed-loop pathology is attributable to the estimation-and-control
architecture rather than to unmodeled forces.

The propagator is cross-validated against an independent oracle: an Orekit
numerical propagator with a degree-4, order-0 gravity field, from identical
initial conditions over the full 30-day campaign duration
(Table~\ref{tab:orekit}). Relative position, the quantity this study uses,
agrees to 2.3~mm RMS (6.9~mm maximum), five orders of magnitude below the
formation scale. Absolute position agrees to 9.1~m RMS. That residual is
consistent with the $\sim$$10^{-9}$ relative difference between the two
libraries' gravitational-constant conventions: a fractional error
$\epsilon$ in $\mu$ offsets the mean motion by $\epsilon/2$, giving an
along-track separation that grows \emph{linearly} as
$a n (\epsilon/2) t \approx 10$~m over 30 days at this altitude, which is
the magnitude observed. It is reported for completeness; the relative state
that this study actually uses is unaffected at the millimetre level.

\subsection{Scenario and campaign design}

The baseline scenario is a near-circular chief at
$a = 7078.137$~km ($\sim$700~km altitude), $e = 0.001$, $i = 98^\circ$,
with 1-km formations, a 30-day duration, and a 60-s step for measurement,
control, and filtering. A trial is declared \emph{diverged} when the
formation-keeping error exceeds a bound of $100\times$ the formation size;
diverged trials are excluded from delta-V statistics and tallied as
failures.

The campaign runs in two tiers. The \emph{screening} tier replaces the
numerical truth with an STM-surrogate truth (KGD plus calibrated white
acceleration) and sweeps the full grid cheaply: 3 controllers $\times$ 3
filter models $\times$ 18 (architecture, $\sigma$) points $\times$ 4
geometries $=$ 648 points at 20 seeds each, or 12{,}960 trials. The
\emph{full} tier reruns the screening-informed grid against the numerical
truth at $N = 100$ seeds, with sigma points pruned to bracket the observed
knees and the redundant GERO geometry dropped: 324 grid points plus five
extended-sigma backfill points, or 32{,}900 trials. Agreement between tiers
validates the surrogate. Per-cell median delta-V rank-correlates at Spearman
$\rho = 0.83$, with numerical-truth medians $7\%$ higher at the median.
Pearson correlation is uninformative at 0.37 because the delta-V
distribution is heavy-tailed, and rank statistics are therefore used
throughout.

Every trial is exactly reproducible. The RNG seed derives from
\texttt{SeedSequence([root, point-hash, trial-index])}, so any single trial
can be regenerated in isolation, and results are written per grid point to
Parquet, making campaigns resumable and incrementally extensible.

\subsection{Knee metric}\label{sec:kneemetric}

The navigation-accuracy ``knee'', the accuracy beyond which further sensor
improvement no longer buys delta-V, is reported with a metric designed after
a naive version proved fragile. That version took the first $\sigma$ whose
median delta-V exceeds $1.1\times$ the tightest-sigma baseline, and on a
coarse sigma grid it agreed between tiers in only 55\% of cells. Per
(controller, architecture, filter, geometry) cell the robust metric reports
three quantities. The first is a \emph{status}, either rising, flat, or
falling, taken from the Spearman trend of median delta-V over feasible sigma
points, where a sigma point is feasible if at least 10 of 100 seeds survive;
this distinguishes ``no knee'' from ``trend inverted'' and from ``all trials
diverged''. The second is a set of threshold knees at 5, 10, 25, and 50\%
delta-V
rise, with a stability flag that marks a verdict \emph{stable} when the 5\%
and 50\% knees agree within one grid step. The
third is a fitted knee from
$\Delta V(\sigma) = \Delta V_0\,(1 + (\sigma/\sigma_k)^p)$, flagged when it
extrapolates beyond the swept range. Infeasible sigma points are marked
explicitly in every figure rather than silently omitted.

\subsection{Reproducibility}

The implementation is pure Python (NumPy/SciPy/CasADi), with a locked
dependency environment (\texttt{uv}), a Docker fallback, continuous
integration running the full verification suite, and a Snakemake DAG that
regenerates every figure in this paper from the committed Parquet data with
one command (\texttt{snakemake -c8 figures}). The knee table follows from
\texttt{scripts/find\_knees.py}, and Table~\ref{tab:orekit} from the
Orekit cross-validation script, which additionally needs a JVM. Code and
data are openly released; the archived version of record is linked in the
data-availability statement.
