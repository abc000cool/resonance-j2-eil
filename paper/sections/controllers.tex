\section{Formation-keeping controllers}\label{sec:controllers}

Three controllers spanning the design space flown or proposed for LEO
formations are implemented. All three act on the \emph{estimated} state
$\hat{x}$; none receives the truth.

\paragraph{Continuous LQR.}
A steady-state linear-quadratic regulator on the Schweighart--Sedwick LTI
plant, with gain from the continuous algebraic Riccati equation, applied as
a zero-order-hold acceleration over each 60-s step and saturated
componentwise at $u_{\max} = 2\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. The
state-weight-to-control-weight ratio sets the control tightness swept in
the perfect-navigation Pareto (Fig.~\ref{fig:perfect}); the campaign uses a
fixed mid-range weighting. A covariance-aware variant, which inflates the
state weight by the filter covariance to soften control action on uncertain
estimates, is implemented for the certainty-equivalence stress test
(Section~\ref{sec:discussion}).

\paragraph{Receding-horizon MPC.}
A model-predictive controller on the KGD ROE plant: at each planning epoch
(once per orbit) it solves a direct transcription over a multi-orbit
horizon --- impulsive velocity increments at fixed per-orbit locations,
quadratic state and delta-V costs, and the saturation bound as a hard
constraint --- using CasADi/IPOPT, and executes the first orbit of the
plan. Planning in ROE space exploits the near-linearity of $J_2$ in these
coordinates \cite{koenig2017stm}.

\paragraph{Closed-form impulsive (Chernick--D'Amico).}
The analytic impulsive scheme of Chernick and D'Amico
\cite{chernick2018closedform,chernick2021reachable}, with pseudo-state
targeting: a single out-of-plane impulse at the $J_2$-refined optimal
argument of latitude corrects the relative inclination vector, and an
in-plane triple-tangential sequence at $J_2$-shifted phases corrects the
remaining elements \cite{gaias2015impulsive,vaddi2005formation}. Plans are
regenerated once per orbit after the previous maneuver sequence completes.
Being deadband-style and analytic, it is the cheapest and loosest of the
three.

\paragraph{Certainty equivalence and delta-V accounting.}
All controllers are certainty-equivalent by construction: $u = u(\hat{x})$,
with the filter covariance entering only the covariance-aware LQR variant.
The estimation error $\hat{x} - x$ therefore acts as an unmodeled input
disturbance whose statistics are set by the navigation architecture --- the
coupling this paper quantifies. Delta-V is accounted as
$\sum \lVert u \rVert \Delta t$ for continuous control plus
$\sum \lVert \Delta v \rVert$ for impulses, annualized by the simulated
duration; trials whose formation-keeping error exceeds a divergence bound
(Section~\ref{sec:method}) are excluded from delta-V statistics and
reported separately as failures.
